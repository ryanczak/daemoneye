# Bug 1 on phase-12: § 5 M6 paragraph asserts a close date that has not happened, contradicting the still-"Active" heading and the phase doc's own Out-of-scope

**Severity:** major
**Status:** open
**Filed:** 2026-07-31

## What's wrong

`docs/architecture.md` § 5, in the paragraph under `### Active milestone — M6
Verification & Hygiene`, now opens:

> Scoped 2026-07-30 (PE sign-off); **closed 2026-07-31.** Milestone README: …

(commit `0f8b7c2`, `docs/architecture.md` line ~378.)

This is false at the time the phase was written: the milestone is not closed.
Phase 12 is still in `review` status when this text was authored, and closing
M6 is an explicit separate human-gated step (`/rexymcp:architect`) that had not
run. The document now contradicts itself in the same section: the heading two
lines above still reads `### Active milestone — M6 Verification & Hygiene`,
while the body prose asserts the milestone is closed.

This also violates the phase doc's own instructions. Phase doc task 3 ("Leave
the close to the human") says: *"Do **not** relabel `### Active milestone —
M6` to `### Shipped — M6`, and do **not** write a retrospective section. M6 is
not closed until the human signs off, and the retrospective is theirs to
approve. Phase 12 makes the section *true*; the close makes it *final*."* And
the phase doc's "Out of scope" section: *"Do not write the M6 retrospective or
relabel the milestone as shipped — human gate."* Asserting a close date in
prose is the same substantive act the heading-relabel prohibition forbids —
it pre-empts a decision reserved for the human gate — and it is not even
accurate at the moment of writing. This is exactly the documented-belief-vs-
reality drift class M6 was scoped to eliminate; landing it in the milestone's
own closing phase would be ironic and would reintroduce a false claim into the
architecture doc immediately after correcting a different one.

## What should happen

The M6 paragraph should state facts as of the time phase 12 lands: scoped
2026-07-30, delivered phase set, phase 12 as the last in-scope phase — but no
assertion that the milestone is closed, and no close date. The heading and the
body must agree that M6 is still active pending the human close step.

## How to fix

In `docs/architecture.md` § 5, remove the `; closed 2026-07-31` clause from
the M6 paragraph's opening sentence, leaving it as (or equivalent):

> Scoped 2026-07-30 (PE sign-off). Milestone README: …

The rest of the paragraph (phase count, 06a/06b split, per-phase delivery
summary, "Phase 12 is the last in-scope phase; the milestone retrospective and
close belong to the human gate") is accurate and should stay.
