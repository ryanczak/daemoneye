# Bug 6 on phase-12: the bar's percentage clips again from 100 %

**Severity:** minor
**Status:** fixed (2026-10-24, def7352a)
**Filed:** 2026-10-24

## What's wrong
`BAR_CHROME` in `src/cli/commands/top_tui.rs` reserves 6 cells: the two
brackets, the space and a two-digit `NN%`. From 100 % the tail is four
characters, so the bar is one wider than the panel and the `%` is cut. On
an 80-column screen the inner width is 78 and `[…] 150%` is 79 long. That
is the `Over` state, the one the red bar exists to show.

## What should happen
Spec item 3: the percentage is printed as it is, including over the cap.

## Root cause
The reserved width assumes a two-digit percentage.
