## What's wrong

The alert severity part has two forms. `alert_parts` (`src/cli/status_bar.rs`,
the `wide` / `compact` pair after `worst_count`) builds the wide form as
`⚠ warn` / `⚠ 2 warn`. It builds the compact form as today's glyph and count,
`⚠1`. So once the words drop, the severity word is gone. The comment above it
says the opposite ("the word stays at every width").

Two tests pin the wrong behaviour:
- `words_drop_all_at_once_before_any_rank_drop` puts `"warn"` in its list of
  words that must be **absent** after the drop.
- `state_and_alert_words_survive_every_drop` asserts only the `⚠` glyph at
  50 columns, not the word.

## What should happen

Phase-09 task 1 says the alert severity part has **one form**. Task 2 says
"The state word and the alert severity word stay at every width the rank
drops leave them". Task 3's table says the severity word stays, with the
count only at two or more. PE decision 7 makes the state word and the alert
severity word exempt from dropping. The PE's 80-column mock-up reads
`ℹ info Watchdog 11h` with every other word dropped.

## Root cause

The executor treated the regression anchor ("compact is byte-for-byte
today's bar") as overriding the exemption. The task-1 sentence names the
alert severity part as one of the two exceptions to that anchor.
